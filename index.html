Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

This policy outlines how our Discord application, MafiaBot, collects, uses, and manages user data in compliance with Discord Developer policies.

1. Data We Collect

We strictly limit data collection to the minimum required information for core functionality. We collect and store:

  • Discord User IDs: A unique string of numbers used to link your application preferences, configurations, or bot data back to your account.

2. Data Usage & Security

Your User ID is securely kept in an encrypted database. It is exclusively used to maintain application persistence. We never share, trade, or sell your data to third parties.

3. Data Deletion & Removal

You have the absolute right to have your data purged from our storage systems. To request immediate deletion of your User ID and all associated records, please join our support community server and contact Linkboi.
